Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · English translation: November 8, 2026

EurekaLife App does not provide a cloud service: there is no EurekaLife cloud account, cloud storage or cloud sync. All records, transcripts, photos, summaries and insights you create in the app stay on your iPhone by default. Apple Watch recordings transfer to your paired iPhone and are stored there.

1. Data we process

  • Records you create: audio, text, photos, transcripts, summaries, tags, reminders and your AI conversations.
  • Sources you choose: with your permission, daily steps and sleep from HealthKit, calendar events for selected dates, and a one-time foreground location when saving a new record. These sources are optional.
  • App settings: appearance, language, model selection, reminder preferences and necessary local indexes. API keys for custom AI providers are stored in Apple Keychain.
  • Purchases: Apple App Store handles one-time purchases. EurekaLife does not receive your payment card number.

2. How data is processed

All records, transcripts, photos, summaries, insights and conversation history stay on your iPhone by default. Apple Watch recordings transfer through the paired connection. EurekaLife does not operate a cloud service that receives, hosts or syncs personal records. Where supported, transcription and Apple on-device models run on your device. Apple may download resources when a language or system model is first used. The app does not send audio or photos to external AI services.

EurekaLife has no proprietary cloud service. Only when you actively connect ChatGPT or configure an OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible provider and initiate a question or summary does selected text go directly to that provider. By default, the app shows the text and asks you to confirm before sending. If you select “Do not ask again” and confirm, subsequent questions and summaries skip that prompt, and limited excerpts from long recordings are sent automatically after transcription to generate titles, until you restore confirmation in Settings → AI Models. Sent content may include selected record excerpts and source text you explicitly allow AI to use; audio and photos are not sent to external AI. The provider’s privacy policy and your account settings govern its processing and retention.

EurekaLife does not use records, HealthKit data or location data for advertising and does not sell that data. EurekaLife does not operate analytics or advertising account services.

3. HealthKit, calendar and location

HealthKit permission is optional and read-only; EurekaLife does not write to HealthKit. Calendar access is optional and read-only. Location is read once in the foreground when the source is enabled and you save a new record; it is not tracked continuously or in the background. You can revoke system permissions in iOS Settings or pause a source in EurekaLife.

When location is enabled and you view a record with coordinates in the foreground, the app may use Apple Maps to resolve a readable address. This may require a network connection and sends coordinates to Apple. Audio, photos and transcripts are not included in the address request. The resolved address stays with the local record and is deleted with it. Pausing the location source stops new address lookups.

Health, calendar and location data support the personal recording and review features you choose. They are not used for advertising, insurance pricing or sale. AI access to a source requires separate permission in app settings. Before sending text to external AI, the app shows it and asks for confirmation by default. You may confirm “Do not ask again” for future sends; this does not expand the sources AI may use. Restore the prompt at any time in AI Model settings.

4. Storage, backup and retention

EurekaLife App does not provide a cloud service; daily records stay on your iPhone. Audio, photos and records in the iOS Documents directory may be included in Apple device backups according to your Apple account and device settings. This is Apple device backup, not a EurekaLife cloud service. To meet health-data storage restrictions, the private Application Support directory, including HealthKit snapshots, AI insights and conversation history, is excluded from iCloud device backups. These snapshots and histories will not be restored from a backup after replacing or restoring a device. With permission, currently available HealthKit data can be read again.

Records stay locally until you delete them in the app or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes files in the app container. Custom AI credentials in Apple Keychain may remain, so remove or disconnect model configurations before uninstalling. Manage Apple device backups and content you sent to external AI providers using the respective Apple or provider tools.

5. Your choices and deletion

  • Revoke microphone, photo, health, calendar or location permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Disable sources, switch to an Apple on-device model or delete custom AI configurations in EurekaLife Settings.
  • Edit or delete records in the app. Deleting the app removes its app data from that device.
  • For data already sent to Apple or your AI provider, use that account or service’s privacy and deletion controls.

6. Third-party services

The app relies on Apple services, including iOS, HealthKit, speech recognition, on-device models, Keychain and App Store. If you enable external AI, your chosen provider processes the content you confirm under its own terms. EurekaLife does not control third-party retention, processing locations or data use. Read the provider’s policy before enabling it.

7. Children and policy updates

EurekaLife is intended for personal daily recording and is not directed at children. We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change and publish the revised version and date here.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact EurekaLife at hello@wiselife.cloud.
